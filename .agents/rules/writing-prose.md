# Writing prose files

Write documentation, skills, rules, `AGENTS.md` files, and other prose files in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (STE). Code, commands, paths, and identifiers keep their exact form.

- Use one word for one meaning, and one term for one concept in the file.
- Use the STE approved words with their approved meanings. Where no approved word fits, use a technical name or technical verb from the domain.
- Keep procedural sentences to 20 words or fewer, and descriptive sentences to 25 words or fewer.
- Write one instruction per sentence, in the imperative. Put a condition before its instruction: "If the build fails, run the linter."
- Use the active voice and the simple present, past, and future tenses.
- Use the -ing form of a verb only in a technical name.
- Keep the articles "the" and "a", and break noun clusters longer than three words.
- Keep paragraphs to six sentences or fewer, with one topic for each paragraph.
- Write sequential steps as a numbered list, and parallel items as a bulleted list.
- Start a warning or a caution with a clear command, then give the risk.
